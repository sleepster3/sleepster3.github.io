Driver Profile

Here to
race.

Isaac began his motorsport journey in the Sodi World Series in 2025. Alongside competing, he is an intern mechanic with ItalCorse Middle East and supports younger drivers through coaching and guidance. These experiences build technical understanding and leadership—but his purpose remains clear: to progress as a racing driver.
