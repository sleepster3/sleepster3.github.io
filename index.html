Driver Profile

Here to
race.

Isaac is a racing driver developing through SWS and Rotax Max Senior. Alongside competing, he works as an intern mechanic with ItalCorse Middle East and supports younger drivers through coaching and guidance. Driver first, mechanic by experience, and mentor by mindset - each role develops the racecraft, technical understanding, and leadership needed to progress.
