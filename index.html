Driver Profile

Here to
race.

Isaac began his motorsport journey in the Sodi World Series in 2025. Alongside his own racing, he works as a mechanic with ItalCorse Middle East and supports younger drivers through coaching and guidance. These experiences build racecraft and technical understanding—but the goal remains clear: to progress as a racing driver.

Personal Statement

Built for the next level.

“I work in the paddock, I learn from every lap, and I help younger drivers grow—but my purpose is clear: I am here to race.”
